Hi team — I'd like to provide an update on the ongoing work to connect the consolidated server to the SHR and OpenCR.

The shape of it is that EMR data should reach the HIE only through the consolidated server. A patient created in iSantePlus is captured by the binlog CDC, lands in the consolidated_db, and from there our SQLMesh pipeline and the FHIR-router mediator map it and push it on to OpenCR for identity and the SHR for clinical data. The EMRs themselves no longer write to the HIE directly; their only interaction with it now is read-only patient search at registration.

The piece we're still waiting on is CHARESS's side — the CDC binlog that captures the EMRs into the consolidated_db. That connection isn't in place for our instances yet, and it's the missing link for a full end-to-end run: until their binlog reader is pointed at the consolidated_db we read from, we can't demonstrate the complete chain of creating a patient in the EMR and watching it appear in OpenCR and the SHR.

On our side, the work is done and verified right up to that boundary. The downstream half of the chain — consolidated_db through the pipeline into OpenCR and the SHR — is built and tested; we've pushed the full test dataset through it successfully. We've also moved the EMRs into a "leaf" mode: we switched off the old paths that used to write directly to OpenCR and the SHR, while keeping patient search working. As part of that we found and fixed a bug in the xds-sender module upstream (now released as v2.6.1) and bundled it into our image.

To be clear about what is and isn't achievable from here: everything from the consolidated_db onward is working on our side — the mapping, the identity matching in OpenCR, and the clinical data landing in the SHR — and the EMR leaf-mode is done. The full end-to-end, however, can't be completed or demonstrated until CHARESS connects their CDC binlog to the consolidated_db; that part is outside our control and is the one real dependency. Separately, there are two things still open on our side: importing a patient from OpenCR into an EMR currently creates duplicate local records, which we need to fix, and OpenCR needs a cleanup of some duplicate golden records.

In short: our half is ready and proven, and the end-to-end is gated on CHARESS wiring up the CDC, after which we can run the full test.
