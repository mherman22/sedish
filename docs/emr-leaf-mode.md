Hi team — I'd like to provide an update on the ongoing work to connect the consolidated server to the SHR and OpenCR.

The architecture we've been building routes all EMR data through the consolidated server. A patient created in iSantePlus is captured by the binlog CDC, lands in the consolidated_db, and from there the SQLMesh pipeline and FHIR-router mediator we developed map the data and push it on to OpenCR for identity and the SHR for clinical content. We've set the iSantePlus instances up to feed the consolidated server as data sources, with their link to the HIE being read-only patient search at registration — which is exactly what the new design calls for.

The one external dependency we're still waiting on is CHARESS's side: the CDC binlog that captures the EMRs into the consolidated_db. That connection isn't in place for our instances yet, and it's the last link needed for a full end-to-end run — until their binlog reader is pointed at the consolidated_db we read from, we can't yet demonstrate the complete chain of creating a patient in the EMR and watching it flow through to OpenCR and the SHR.

On our side we've done and verified the work right up to that boundary. We built and tested the downstream half of the chain — consolidated_db through the pipeline into OpenCR and the SHR — and successfully pushed the full test dataset through it. We also reconfigured the iSantePlus instances to route through the consolidated server rather than push straight to the HIE, while keeping patient search working. Along the way we found a bug in the xds-sender module, fixed it and got the fix merged upstream (released as v2.6.1), and bundled that version into our image.

To be clear on what is and isn't achievable from here: everything from the consolidated_db onward is working on our side — the mapping, the identity matching in OpenCR, and the clinical data landing in the SHR — and the EMR-side reconfiguration is done. The full end-to-end, though, can't be completed or demonstrated until CHARESS connects their CDC binlog to the consolidated_db; that's the one real dependency and it sits outside our control. Separately, we have two items still to close on our side: importing a patient from OpenCR into an EMR currently creates duplicate local records, which we're fixing, and OpenCR needs a cleanup of some duplicate golden records.

In short, we've built and proven our half of the integration; the end-to-end is now gated on CHARESS wiring up the CDC, after which we can run the complete test.
